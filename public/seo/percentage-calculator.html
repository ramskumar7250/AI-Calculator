What is a Percentage?

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. The percentage symbol is %.

For example, 25% means 25 out of 100, or 0.25.

Percentage Formula

To calculate a percentage of a number, use this formula:

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

To find a specific percentage of a number:

Result = Number × Percentage ÷ 100

Percentage Calculation Examples

What is 20% of 500?

500 × 20 ÷ 100 = 100

What is 15% of 2,000?

2,000 × 15 ÷ 100 = 300

What is 12% of 50,000?

50,000 × 12 ÷ 100 = 6,000

Percentage Increase

Percentage increase tells you how much a value has increased compared with its original value.

Percentage Increase = ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example: A price increases from ₹1,000 to ₹1,200.

Increase = ₹200

Percentage increase = (200 ÷ 1,000) × 100 = 20%

Percentage Decrease

Percentage decrease shows how much a value has decreased compared with its original value.

Percentage Decrease = ((Original Value − New Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Example: A price decreases from ₹1,000 to ₹800.

Decrease = ₹200

Percentage decrease = (200 ÷ 1,000) × 100 = 20%

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide the result by 100. For example, 20% of 500 is 500 × 20 ÷ 100 = 100.

What is 20% of 500?

20% of 500 is 100.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value, and multiply by 100.
